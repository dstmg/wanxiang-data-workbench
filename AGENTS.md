# Project safety

- Read README.md and USAGE.md before changes. Keep the service bound to 127.0.0.1; no public deployment or credential extraction.
- Preserve original exports. Do not upload reports, product catalogs, reviews, browser profiles, screenshots, config.local.json or secrets.
- Test with temporary data and review directories; do not mutate the user's default records. Cloud AI analysis of private data needs explicit informed approval.
- No automatic budget/plan modifications. Missing is not zero, ROI is not profit, exact IDs only. Do not infer unavailable cross-dimension relations.
- Own additions are MIT. Retain Tremor Apache-2.0 notices; do not relabel upstream components as MIT. Clearly document modified derivative files.
